    Can I trust this list

    How it works

    Deal Radar reads public records and trade news about senior housing, ties each fact to the building it is about, and ranks the buildings that show distress. Every lead links back to what it rests on.

    Four steps, no black box

      What gets filtered out

      Most buildings never reach the list.

        NOT FOUND, never guessed

        A fact appears only when a source states it. Anything no source states is written NOT FOUND, in amber, instead of being estimated: a missing buyer, unit count, phone number or website stays NOT FOUND until a source names it.

        Facts that are deduced rather than stated (a parent company matched by brand, a state worked out from a city) are labelled as inferred. Contact details are business lines only: never personal names, mobile numbers or personal e-mail.

        How a building is scored

        Fixed rules, the same for every building. Each lead shows its own breakdown.
