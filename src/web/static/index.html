How a building is scored

Contact details

Each lead shows the property's main phone line, its public website and its street address, so you can reach out directly. Only business contact points are shown (never personal names, mobile numbers or personal e-mail), and each one links to the public page it came from with the date it was checked. When a source does not give one, it reads NOT FOUND.

NOT FOUND, never guessed

A fact is shown only when a source states it. Anything no source states is written NOT FOUND (highlighted amber) instead of being estimated: a missing buyer, unit count, city, phone or link stays NOT FOUND until a source names it. Inferred facts (a parent company matched by brand, a state deduced from a city) are labelled as inferred.

Public sources only. Everything on this site comes from public filings, government data sets and published articles, and each lead lists its links. Leads whose evidence is not public are not shown.
